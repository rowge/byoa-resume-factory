# Questionnaire

This is the work. Better as a **conversation** with the agent than a silent
form — you will remember stories while you talk. Typing here is fine too.
Messy, incomplete, voice-dictated: all fine. **UNKNOWN** or **approx** beats
a guess.

The more honest you are, and the more incidents you can remember, the better
every resume gets. More stories → better. That is the whole kit.

Agent: walk this **out loud**, eras first, then stories on each era. Do not
dump the entire file as one message.

## Tracks (optional — skip if you have one hunt)

A **track** is a named hunt: any mix of title, seniority, cities, domain,
pay, pace. Not a vibe label unless you want one.

Examples (delete when you have your own):

- **HPC South, senior** — HPC / cluster work, senior level, Austin or Nashville.
  You’ll take the senior seat where the level is real.
- **HPC Hawaii, mid** — same kind of work, Hawaii. Mid-tier is fine; you
  want to live there and you can probably get it.

Same skills, different trade (level vs place). Your tracks can be anything.

For each track:

```

Where (cities, remote, on-site/hybrid):
Other criteria (domain, “no finance,” comp, whatever):
```

## Stories

Fewer than you think to *start*; more than you think to *win*. Enough
scales with **years worked** (CONTEXT eras), not job hops:

- ~0–3 years: keep going toward ~6–8; do not hunt for 15
- ~3–8 years: keep going past ~12
- ~8+ years: keep going past ~15; shaky under that

Dump what you remember. STAR if you can. A number you can defend is gold.
No number is better than a fake number.

Prompts (skip what doesn’t fit):

- A time you stopped the same outage from repeating
- A migration or replacement that was stuck until you moved it
- Something you automated that ate real hours
- On-call: a bad night, what you did, what changed after
- Scale: the largest fleet, cluster, or dataset you actually operated
- A time you were wrong, and how you found out
- Mentoring / leading without a fancy title
- A thing you built that people still used after you left
- Origin story of how you learned this work (if unusual)

Paste extras here or in `stories/INBOX.md`.

## Two extras (end of setup)

Agent: ask these in chat, don’t skip past them. If they don’t answer, both
stay **on**. Write the answers into CONTEXT.

**1. Application-form drafts**  
A lot of Apply pages aren’t just resume + cover letter. They ask “Why do you
want to work here?”, “Tell us about a time you…”, essays, and similar. If I
can open that form (or the posting makes the questions obvious), I’ll put
**short draft answers in chat** so you’re not staring at a blank Workday box.
I won’t paste the cover letter again — that’s a file.

Do you want those drafts? **Yes / no.**

**2. Pay**  
If the form asks for expected salary, desired comp, or a number, I’ll suggest
a **range** for *that role*, using your situation if you told me it, plus a
plain caveat (not an offer prediction). I won’t volunteer pay if the form
doesn’t ask, unless you ask.

Do you want any of that money talk? **Yes / no.**

Fit scores, story nudges, tracks, and the status board stay on. They’re why
this works. Change them at your peril.
